How we work

With your child, or with you

1. Diagnosis of the real person

We do not start with the curriculum. We start by understanding how your child thinks, what blocks them and what motivates them, or the same process for you, if you are an adult or a senior.

2. Full afternoons, in small groups

Sessions of hours, not 45 minutes squeezed between two lessons. Time to get it wrong, try again, and actually learn.

3. Dashboard shared with you

See what has changed without waiting for the report card. Every session is logged and visible on a digital dashboard.

4. Direct conversation

We talk to you about what is actually changing. No generic reports, no bureaucratic language.

This is not a medical, psychological or psychiatric consultation. We do not replace those professionals, we complement them. We are the practical link that is missing between what school does not solve, what the psychologist has no time to teach and what nobody explains to parents.

Specialised Consulting for Parents

The problem we know well

Grades in free fall. A lack of motivation no one seems able to fix. Diagnoses that frighten more than they help. The feeling that school is not doing enough, that the psychologist keeps saying the same things, that the doctor has no time to explain.

If you have found your way here, you have probably already tried several paths. We know this situation well, and we know what is missing.

What we do

Direct consulting with André da Silva, McMinsky's founder, with a PhD in neuroscience and over a decade of intensive hands-on experience with challenging youth and their parents.

  • Deep analysis of the academic, behavioural and family situation
  • Concrete, personalised action plan, not generic advice
  • Neuroscience-based strategies that actually work in practice
  • Guidance on the education system and the best available options
  • Understanding your child's brain and profile: how they think, learn and what blocks them
  • Approaches and techniques most professionals do not know or do not apply
  • Support for handling difficult everyday situations at home
  • Practical resources you can use immediately

Who it's for

  • Parents of children or teens with poor school results
  • Situations that need an urgent, competent plan
  • Neurodivergent children and teens (ADHD, dyslexia, autism, giftedness, etc.)
  • Chronic lack of motivation or school refusal
  • Difficult behaviour at home or at school
  • Parents who want to better understand what is happening and what they can do differently
  • Anyone who needs an experienced, honest second opinion

What it is NOT

This is not a medical, psychological or psychiatric consultation. We do not replace those professionals, we complement them. We are the practical link that is missing between what school does not solve, what the psychologist has no time to teach and what nobody explains to parents.

Why McMinsky

Over a decade in the field, with hundreds of young people and families. Advanced training in neuroscience. Deep knowledge of education systems. Real, not theoretical, experience with the most challenging cases. And above all, results.

There is hope. And there is method.
