The Shortcut: Superpowers Nobody Teaches You

This is not about learning to use ChatGPT for basic questions. Everyone does that. What you will learn here is what smart people are already doing without telling anyone — how to use AI to do in 10 minutes what takes others hours.

University assignments? The AI structures, researches and drafts — you review and submit. Work reports? Spreadsheets that would take a day are done in minutes. Professional emails? You write like you have an MBA. And nobody needs to know how you do everything so well and so fast.

It is almost like cheating. Except it is not — it is just knowing how to use the right tools. And we are going to teach you exactly how.

  • Stop depending on your body to earn money — and start using your head (with AI’s help)
